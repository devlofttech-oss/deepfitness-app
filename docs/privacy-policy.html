What we do not collect

  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or photos from your device.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or analytics SDK.
  • No payment or financial information — the app does not process payments.
  • No microphone or camera capture. Profile pictures are chosen from a fixed set of built-in icons.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show you the workout plan your trainer assigned, and to record what you completed.
  • To calculate your progress statistics — streaks, adherence, personal bests, and the muscle-group progress map.
  • To let your assigned trainer review your progress and adjust your plan.
  • To respond to you when you contact support.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit scoring, or automated decisions with legal effects. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. Who can see your data

Access is enforced at the database level with row-level security rules, not just in the app:

  • You can see your own data.
  • Your assigned trainer can see your profile, plans, logs, measurements, and notes. This is the core purpose of the app.
  • Other members cannot see your data, and trainers cannot see members who are not assigned to them.
  • Our administrators may access data where strictly necessary to operate the service, investigate a fault, or comply with the law.

6. Legal basis for processing

Where the GDPR or similar law applies to you, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the coaching service you signed up for.
  • Explicit consent — for health and fitness data, which is a special category of personal data. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and fitness data for as long as your account exists, because the app's purpose is to show change over time. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain records to comply with law.

Deleting your account cascades to your profile, measurements, exercise logs, notes, water and diet logs, and assigned plans.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — including without installing the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate — much of this you can edit yourself in the app's Profile screen.
  • Delete your data (see section 8).
  • Object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent.
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email deepfitnessgym2025@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity from the email or phone number registered to the account before we act.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed by our authentication provider; they are never stored in readable form.
  • Row-level security policies restrict every query to the rows that the signed-in account is entitled to see.
  • Trainer accounts are provisioned by administrators, not by public self-registration.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

11. Children
