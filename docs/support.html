Support

Help with the Deep Fitness app — sign-in trouble, missing workout plans, and account questions.

Email support

We reply within [e.g. 2 business days]. To help us help you, include your account email, your device model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

I don't have a workout plan yet

Plans are assigned by your gym trainer, not created by you. If your Today screen is empty, your trainer has not assigned a session for that date yet — contact your gym.

"Check your internet connection and try again"

The app could not reach our servers. Check that you are online and try again. If it keeps happening while other apps work normally, email us — it may be an outage on our side rather than your connection.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot Password? on the login screen and enter the email address registered to your account. You will receive a reset link. Password reset requires an email address — if you registered with a phone number only, contact your gym.

"Use the member login for this account"

You used the wrong sign-in screen. Members sign in on the main login screen; trainers use the separate Are you a trainer? Log in link. Your account type determines which one works.

How do I become a trainer on the app?

Trainer accounts are created by gym administrators only and cannot be self-registered. Ask your gym to set one up.

My weight or measurements look wrong

Measurements may be recorded either by you or by your assigned trainer. You can check your unit preference (kg or lb) under Profile → Settings → Units. If a figure is still wrong, ask your trainer to correct it, or email us.

How do I delete my account?

See the account deletion page. You can request it by email without installing the app.
