Delete your account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Deep Fitness account and the data attached to it. You do not need to install the app to make this request.

Applies to the Deep Fitness app (package com.deepfitness.app), published by [YOUR LEGAL ENTITY NAME].

Request deletion by email

Send us an email from the email address registered to your Deep Fitness account. Sending from the registered address is how we verify the request is really yours.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your mail app

Send an email manually to deepfitnessgym2025@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Deep Fitness - Account Deletion Request
  • The name on your account
  • The email address or phone number you use to sign in

What happens next

  1. We confirm your request came from the registered email or phone number on the account.
  2. We delete your account and personal data.
  3. We email you to confirm once deletion is complete.

We action requests within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed — your workout history, logs, and measurements cannot be recovered afterwards.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in credentials and authentication record
  • Your profile — name, email, phone, gender, age, height, and fitness goal
  • Your body measurements, including weight and body fat history
  • Every exercise log you recorded — sets, repetitions, and weights
  • Your exercise notes and any trainer notes attached to you
  • Your water intake and diet/meal logs
  • The workout plans and scheduled sessions assigned to you
  • Your app settings — notification preference and preferred units

Members of a gym: deleting your Deep Fitness account removes your data from this app only. It does not cancel your gym membership, and it does not delete any separate records your gym keeps outside the app. Contact your gym directly for those.

Delete some data without closing your account

If you would rather keep your account but remove specific information, email us at deepfitnessgym2025@gmail.com and tell us what you want removed — for example your measurement history or your exercise notes. You can also edit most profile fields yourself in the app under Profile.
