About the app

Deep Fitness connects gym members with their trainers. Trainers build and assign date-specific workout plans from a shared exercise library; members see the day's session, log sets, reps, and weights, and track progress through streaks, personal bests, and a muscle-group progress map.

Your data is visible only to you and your assigned trainer. We do not sell data and the app carries no advertising.
