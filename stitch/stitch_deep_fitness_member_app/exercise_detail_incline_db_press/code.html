Form Instructions & Setup

3 Cues
1
Bench Angle & Grip

Set bench to exactly 30 degrees. Retract shoulder blades firmly into the pad. Plant feet flat on the Mysore studio rubber flooring to create strong leg drive.

2
Controlled Eccentric

Lower the dumbbells in a strict 3-second descent until elbows reach roughly 45–60 degrees from torso. Feel a deep, active stretch across the clavicular pectoralis.

3
Explosive Arc Drive

Press upward smoothly along a gentle arc, stopping just short of full elbow lockout to maintain constant metabolic tension on the chest fibers.

tips_and_updates
Deep Fitness Coaching Cue

Never clang dumbbells at the peak lockout. Keep wrists vertically stacked over forearms throughout the entire stroke.
